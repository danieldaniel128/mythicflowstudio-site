Apex Dragon: Delete your data

For the Android game Apex Dragon: Evolution .io by Mythic Flow Studio (package com.daniel.apexdragon).

The game has no accounts: no email, no password, no login. What it uses online is a random player ID and the name you type, as described in our privacy policy. You can remove your data in two ways.

Ask us to delete it

  1. Email hello@mythicflowstudio.com with the subject "Apex Dragon - delete my data".
  2. Tell us the name you use in the game and roughly when you last played, so we can find your data.
  3. We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm. Once the request is done we delete your email too.

Delete it from your phone yourself

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → Apex Dragon → Storage and tap Clear data, or uninstall the game.
  2. This removes your random player ID, your player name and your settings from the phone. If you play online again, the game starts with a new random ID.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: your random player ID and its sign-in record at Unity, our online service provider, and everything linked to it.
  • Never stored: match details (positions, scores, the players in a match) exist only while the match is open, and we do not store IP addresses.
  • Kept: technical diagnostics that Unity's services keep without your player ID, under the Unity Privacy Policy, and Google Play's anonymous install and crash statistics. Neither can be linked back to you.
