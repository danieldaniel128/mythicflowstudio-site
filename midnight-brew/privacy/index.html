Midnight Brew: Privacy Policy

Applies to the Android game Midnight Brew (package com.mythicflowstudio.midnightbrew). Effective 8 November 2026.

Midnight Brew is made by Mythic Flow Studio ("we", "us"). This page explains what information the game uses. If you have a question, write to hello@mythicflowstudio.com.

In short

  • The game collects no personal information. Nothing you do in the game is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no purchases.
  • Your progress and settings are saved on your phone only.

1. Information the game uses

  • Data saved on your phone. Your best scores, nights cleared, ingredients, brewed potions and settings (such as sound on or off) are stored on your device only. They never leave it.
  • No network use. The game itself makes no network requests. It works the same with no internet connection.
  • Crash and install statistics. Google Play gives us anonymous, combined statistics about installs, crashes and app performance under Google's Privacy Policy. We don't add any analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own.

2. Sharing

We don't collect personal information, so there is nothing for us to share or sell.

3. Your choices

  • Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, removes everything it stored on your phone.
  • For any question about your privacy, email hello@mythicflowstudio.com.

4. Children

Midnight Brew is not directed at children under 13, and it does not collect personal information from anyone.

5. Changes to this policy

If the game starts using information in a new way, for example if ads are added, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches players.
