The studio

One clear idea per game, polished until it flows.

Mythic Flow Studio is an independent game studio making games for Android. Every project starts from one idea you can grasp in seconds, like bending a beam of light or growing a dragon, and we build outward until it feels right in your hands.

The genres change from game to game. The standard doesn't.

  • 01

    One idea, done well

    Each game is built around a single core mechanic, and everything else serves it.

  • 02

    Readable at a glance

    Clear visuals and controls, designed to make sense on a phone screen.

  • 03

    Built in the open

    Every clip on this site is captured in-game. No mockups, no pre-rendered trailers.
