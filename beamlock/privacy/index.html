BEAMLOCK: Privacy Policy

Applies to the Android game BEAMLOCK: Laser Puzzle (package com.danielkaarur.beamlock). Effective 8 November 2026 (first version 7 November 2026).

BEAMLOCK is made by Mythic Flow Studio ("we", "us"). This page explains what information the game uses, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. If you have a question, write to hello@mythicflowstudio.com.

In short

  • No account, no email address, no password. Your progress is saved on your phone.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, files, phone number or payment details.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob: ads you choose to watch for a reward, and some ads between levels. AdMob uses your device's advertising ID and similar information to show and measure ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent first.
  • Gameplay statistics are sent only if you turn on Share play data. It is off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time.
  • We never sell your information.

1. Information the game uses

  • Data saved on your phone. Your level progress, stars, coins, daily streak, settings and your answer to the play-data question are stored on your device only.
  • Advertising. The game shows ads served by Google AdMob: rewarded ads that you choose to watch (for example for a hint or to skip a level) and ads shown between levels. To choose, show and measure ads and to prevent ad fraud, the AdMob software in the game sends Google your device's advertising ID, your IP address (from which Google works out your approximate location), basic device and app information (such as the phone model, Android version, language and game version) and how you interact with the ads (views and taps). Whether ads are personalised depends on your choices: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested.
  • Gameplay statistics (only if you agree). The first time you open the menu, the game asks whether you want to share play data, and the answer starts as no. If you turn it on, here or later with Share play data in the game's options, the game sends Unity Analytics what happens in play: which levels you start, finish, reset, quit or skip, how long they take, how many moves and tries, hints used, daily challenges and streak rewards, and items picked in the shop. Unity Analytics adds a random ID it creates for this installation of the game, basic device and app information (such as the phone model, Android version, language and game version) and your approximate location (your country, worked out from your IP address). It is not linked to your name, email or Google account. We use it only to find levels that are too hard or unclear and to improve the game.
  • Purchases. The shop sells coin, hint and skip packs and Remove Ads through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The game receives a record of what you bought so it can deliver it, and Google Play lets the game restore Remove Ads if you reinstall. If you turned on Share play data, which product you bought is also recorded with the gameplay statistics.
  • Crash and install statistics. Google Play gives us anonymous, combined statistics about installs, crashes and app performance under Google's Privacy Policy.

2. How we use it

To run the game and save your progress, to show ads through Google AdMob, which keeps the game free, and, if you agreed, to see how levels play so we can make the game better. We do not sell or rent your information.

3. Who it is shared with

  • Google, which distributes the game through Google Play and serves its ads through AdMob. Google uses the advertising information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Unity Technologies, which provides Unity Analytics, only if you turned on Share play data. Unity processes this data for us; see the Unity Privacy Policy.
  • Authorities, only if the law requires it.

4. How long it is kept

  • Data saved on your phone stays there until you clear the game's data or uninstall it.
  • Gameplay statistics are kept in Unity Analytics under Unity's retention rules, and we can delete them on request.
  • Advertising information is kept by Google under its own retention rules (see the Google Privacy Policy).

5. Your choices

  • Turn Share play data off in the game's options to stop gameplay statistics from being sent.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (on most phones: Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads).
  • Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, removes everything stored on your phone.
  • To ask what we hold about you, or to have your gameplay statistics deleted, email hello@mythicflowstudio.com. Because the game has no accounts, we may need your help to identify your data.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, we process gameplay statistics on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in the game's options. Advertising data is processed by Google on the basis of the consent you give (or refuse) in the consent form. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

6. Children

BEAMLOCK is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

7. Security

The ad and analytics services the game uses send data over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep the information we handle to the minimum the game needs.

8. Changes to this policy

If the game starts using information in a new way, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches players.
