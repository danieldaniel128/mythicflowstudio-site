The idea

Puzzle games get padded — timers, lives, ads between thoughts. We build the opposite: a fixed board, a fixed toolbox, one correct route, and the satisfaction of finding it yourself.

Every level we ship carries its own verified solution before it reaches a phone. If a puzzle can be beaten by accident, we consider it broken — not clever.
